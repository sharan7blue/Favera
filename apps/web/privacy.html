What we collect

  • Request form answers. What you type into our Tally form, such as your name, business name, website and what you want from a report.
  • Your email address and anything you write to us in emails.
  • Search Console data, only if you or your client grants access or gives us an export. We use it only to build the report you asked for.

We ask only for what is listed above, and we do not set out to collect personal data about your clients beyond what appears in the Search Console data you choose to share.

Our role: controller and processor

For your form answers and emails, Faevra is the "controller" under UK GDPR: we decide how and why that data is used.

For a client's Search Console export that an agency gives us, Faevra is the agency's "processor" and the agency is the controller. In that case we:

  • use the data only on the agency's instructions, to produce the report;
  • delete or return it on request;
  • keep it secure; and
  • use no sub-processors beyond those listed in "Who else handles your data" below.

Business email and your right to opt out

We may email business contacts at limited companies and LLPs about our service. The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) allow this for corporate bodies, and we do not email sole traders or ordinary partnerships this way. Every such email says who we are and how to stop them. Reply "stop" or write to privacy@faevra.io and we will not email you again. We keep a short do-not-email list so that we remember. You can also object under data protection law at any time.

Data you pass on from your clients

If you give us Search Console access or exports that belong to your client, you tell us you are allowed to. We act as your processor for that data, as set out above, and we will not use it for our own purposes.

How long we keep it

  • Form answers and emails: up to 12 months.
  • Search Console exports: deleted 30 days after the report is delivered.
  • Do-not-email records: kept so we honour your request.
  • Invoice and tax records, if and when we issue invoices: for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or move it to another service. You can object to business email at any time and we will stop.

How to ask for deletion

Email privacy@faevra.io with the subject "Delete my data" and the email address you used. We will reply in writing and delete your data, except records we must keep by law. The law requires us to respond without undue delay and at the latest within one month. If your request is complex we may extend that by up to two further months, and if so we will tell you within the first month and explain why. If you granted Search Console access, you can also remove our access yourself in your Google account settings.
