- DigitilizeWeb (https://www.digitilizeweb.com/): "80+" staff (site), and SEO is not its main service.
- The Content Guild Ltd (https://www.sortlist.co.uk/agency/the-content-guild-ltd): one person on the profile, too small.
- AH Web SEO (https://ahwebseo.co.uk): one person ("I personally handle all campaigns") and no company number. Sole-trader pattern.
- Go Up (https://www.goup.co.uk/contact-us/): legal form not stated and no company number. Left out; could be checked at CH if the list needs a reserve.
- Atomic Digital Marketing (https://atomicdigitalmarketing.co.uk/): legal form and size not stated. Reserve.
- Bird (https://www.bird.co.uk/): site blocks the fetch (robots).
- Mentor Digital (https://www.mentordigital.co.uk/): SEO not named as a service; legal form not stated.
- Lnet Digital (https://www.lnetdigital.co.uk/): SEO not a main service.

## Notes for the project manager

- Rows 1 to 8 are the safest for the Ltd test. Rows 9 to 13 need the status check before any use. Rows 14 and 15 need a CH check first.
- Several rows are full-service agencies where SEO is one service. The "SEO agency or consultancy" test is met on the site text but is weaker for rows 3, 6, 7, 8, 11, 13 and 14.
- No size figures came from a third-party source. "Size if known" is site text only.
- Companies House results are only as current as the search tool showed. Status for rows 9 to 13 is open.
